Eusocial

A place for parents living with perinatal mental health struggles — before, during, and long after the first year — to say what happened, anonymously. No names. No accounts. No judgment. You choose: let your words go, or let them count.

Why this exists
1 in 5 women experience a perinatal mental health condition — most never receive treatment. For Black women, it's closer to 1 in 3 — and they're half as likely to receive care. Indigenous women are three times more likely to have their needs go unmet. Women of color overall are diagnosed later, if at all. 2SLGBTQIA+ parents face some of the highest unmet needs — highest among transgender parents. Every story here documents why — and what must change.

Because stories are anonymous, we can't identify or remove a specific story once shared — please share only what you're comfortable having used. It's out of you now.

Your words were real, and now they belong to the night sky. What you're carrying is heavy, and it is not your fault. People get to the other side of this — every day, with support.

A real person, if you want one

Sometimes you need a human who's been there. Eusocial offers free, text-based peer check-ins from a trained and certified peer supporter with lived experience. Not therapy, not medical care, no records kept — just someone in your corner.
